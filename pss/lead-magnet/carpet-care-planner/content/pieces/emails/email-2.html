Why entries and walkways come first.

When you walk the floor with your carpet care planner, you'll find some zones need more attention than others. Here's why.

Entries Work Hardest

 

Everyone walks through them, and they carry dirt in from outside. They're usually the zones you'll mark heavy, and the first ones people see when they walk in.

Meeting Rooms Can Wait

 

A meeting room used a few times a week wears far more slowly. It can sit on a longer schedule.

Signs To Look For

 
✓
Traffic lanes and entries look dirty, and vacuuming no longer fixes it
✓
Spills or gum still there after the last clean
✓
The busiest spots look worn before the rest
