How Often Is Often Enough?

If you look after an office, a strata building, a managed property or a commercial site, you might be asking the same things.

“How often should our office carpet actually be cleaned?”You don't want to leave it too long, or pay for cleans you don't need.
“The traffic lanes and entries look dirty and vacuuming doesn’t fix it any more.”Some zones need attention long before others. The planner helps you see which.
“I need something to show the owner, committee or head office.”The planner ends with a one-page plan you can forward, so the schedule is easy to explain.

What's In The Planner

Seven short parts, in the order you'd walk your site.

1
Your siteYou note how many people use it, the days it's open, whether it's carpet tiles or broadloom, and roughly when it was last cleaned.
2
Your zonesYou list each area (entries, walkways and corridors, desks, meeting rooms, common areas and lifts) and tick how busy each one is: light, medium or heavy.
3
How often for each zoneThe minimum for a light zone: a low traffic office of 5 to 10 people, cleaned at least once a year. A starting guide for busier zones, confirmed with me on the free walk through your site.
4
Signs a zone needs it soonerA short list to look for when you walk the floor, such as traffic lanes and entries looking dirty when vacuuming no longer fixes it.
5
Fitting it around the workYou tick when a clean can happen: after hours, weekends or nights, or over a shutdown.
6
Your plan on one pageYour schedule for each zone in one table, ready to forward to the owner, head office or committee.
7
Your next stepA free quote after a walk through your site, where I check the plan against your floors. My number is there too, and a way to ask me a question.

Good To Know Before You Start

Is the planner really free?
Yes. It's yours to keep and use, with no obligation.
Will it tell me exactly how often to clean?
It gives you the minimum for light zones and a starting guide for busier zones, such as entries and walkways. The final schedule is set with me on the free walk through your site.
Can the clean fit around our work?
Yes. Offices get done after hours or over a shutdown, so nobody is stepping over hoses in the middle of the working day.
What does the owner or committee see afterwards?
A written report with photos when the job is done, from the one person who walks your site, does the job and writes the report.
