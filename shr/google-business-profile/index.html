6

If Google Asks For A Video

Only needed if Google asked for a video in step 5. If you're already verified, tick this step and move on.

Google often asks a home-based business for a short video to show the business is real. There's no rush: take the time to get ready, then film it once.

Have These Three Things Ready

  • Where you are: a street sign close to your home. Your house number and front door are not needed.
  • Your work set-up: your desk and laptop with your website open, plus anything with your business name on it, such as a business card or your Capability Statement.
  • That it's your business: one document showing Simply Human Resources, such as an invoice or your ABN registration. Cover any bank or tax numbers.

How To Film It

  • Use your phone, in daylight, and hold it steady.
  • One take from start to finish. No stopping and no editing.
  • At least 30 seconds. One to two minutes is ideal.
  • Start at the street sign, walk inside to your desk, show your laptop and business items, then finish on the document.
  • No faces are needed, yours included. No talking is needed either.
  • Keep other people, and anything private, out of the shot.

Practise Once

  • Walk the route with your normal phone camera first, before you record in Google.
  • Watch it back. Can you read the street sign, see your set-up and read the business name on the document?

Record The Real One

  • Open your profile on your phone, tap Get verified, and choose the video option.
  • Film it exactly as you practised, in one take, and upload it straight away.
  • Google can take up to five business days to review it.
