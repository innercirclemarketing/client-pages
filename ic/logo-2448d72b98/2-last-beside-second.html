The last version beside a second version

From what the design check found, I had a second version built of the five that were left. Each pair below shows the last version on the left and the second version on the right, with short notes on what changed and why.

What I decided here

Number 1, the lower case one, in its second version. It reads as one name, and it is the only style where "marketing" stays readable at website header size.

The one thing I didn't like was the deeper gold dot on the light background. To my eye it looks a bit too brown. That is what the third page is about.

Changed in every second version

  1. A little more room in "inner circle". The letters were set very tight, and "rc" and "cl" in "circle" were close to touching. Small or printed, "cl" can start to read as "d".
  2. The two dots are slightly larger and sit a touch higher. In plain black or white the dots are the only thing that makes this a logo and not just typed words, so they need to look deliberate.
  3. A deeper gold dot on light backgrounds. The bright gold was fading into the cream, and almost vanished in grey print. The navy look keeps the bright gold, where it already stands out.
