Where it started: twelve ways to stack it

This is the first of three short pages showing how I got to the logo. I started with "marketing" centred under "inner circle" and tried it twelve ways: capitals and lower case, wider and closer, a softer colour, and a short line between the two. Each is shown on the light look and the navy look, large and at website header size.

What I decided here

I had a design check run over all twelve, then took four out and kept working on the rest.

The two widest ones (1a and 2a) went, because with that much space the eye spells the word out letter by letter. The two heavier ones (1d and 2d) went too, because "marketing" started to compete with the name. They are marked "Left out" below.
