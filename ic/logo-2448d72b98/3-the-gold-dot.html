The gold dot, and where it has landed

The last thing to settle was the second dot. The deeper gold looked a bit too brown to me on the light background, so I had the same logo shown with four golds: on cream, sand, white and navy, large and at website header size, with a grey print check at the end of the small row.

What I decided here

B, the richer gold. It has the same brightness as the gold I started with, with more colour in it, so it stands out on cream without turning brown.

White is the hardest ground for any yellow, so I will simply not use the logo on white.
