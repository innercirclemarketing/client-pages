Inner Circle Marketing: the touch-ups

Megan, thank you for your thoughts on the look. I tried both of your ideas. The bright blue felt like a little too much for me, and the ticks in the logo's yellow gave it the pop it needed, so I've gone with those.

I've also added a little depth: small lines, soft shadows and very subtle gradients, each one only a slight shift of its own colour. Would you mind having a quick look? There is a tick and a box under each part, and the button at the bottom sends your thoughts back to me.

Part 1

The light look

The ticks are now in the logo's yellow. There is a short orange line above each heading, a fine yellow line at the start of the opening paragraph, and fine dividing lines inside the boxes. The warm white background deepens very slightly towards the bottom.

This is just an example of how it could look. It isn't a link.
Do the yellow ticks and the small lines sit well for you? Is anything too much, or not enough?
Part 2

The dark look, for strong sections

The same details on navy, for the parts of a page that need to stand out. The navy shifts very slightly from one corner to the other and stays navy all the way across.

How does the dark version feel beside the light one?
Part 3

The gradient colours, in pairs

Each gradient uses two shades of the same colour. They sit side by side on top, with the gradient they make underneath.
